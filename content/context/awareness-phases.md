# Awareness phases (from Alejandra's SEO audit, Jun 2026)

Source: "Audit report for Leasey" (Coda), SEO Content section. This is the funnel framework the content system uses to balance topics across awareness levels. The calendar-planner uses it to make sure we are not only writing top-of-funnel.

## The 5 awareness levels (the buyer journey)

1. **Unaware.** Doesn't know software exists to automate leasing. Manages vacancies and leads manually with email + spreadsheets.
2. **Problem-aware.** Has heard of "PropTech" or "AI leasing" but isn't clear it applies to their operation or is worth the investment.
3. **Solution-aware.** Knows the benefits of automating leasing (reduce vacancy, scale without headcount, respond 24/7), but doesn't know which tool to choose or how to evaluate options.
4. **Product-aware.** Knows platforms exist (AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi, ShowMojo, Funnel, EliseAI, Leasey) and compares features, integrations, pricing.
5. **Most-aware.** Knows Leasey.AI, close to deciding. Needs a push (demo, relevant case study, pilot).

## Where each phase lives

- **Phases 1, 2, 3:** blog, research pages, tools (calculators).
- **Phase 4:** feature pages, comparison pages, scenario pages.
- **Phase 5:** /pricing/, /book-demo/, case studies, testimonials.

## The finding that matters for content

The site's content is heavily phase 1-2 (definitions, concepts). High impressions but low CTR and far from conversion. **The gap is phases 3 and 4, the decision stage, where demos actually move.** Competitors (AppFolio, Buildium, ShowMojo, Entrata, EliseAI) are taking that band with comparisons, ROI content, and decision-stage pages.

Highest-volume keywords sit in phase 2 ("what is property management software", "best property management software", "ai leasing software", "lease ai"), but the bulk of CLICKS comes from phase 5 (brand searches: leasey ai, leasy ai, leasey).

## Content rule for the system

- **Keep phase 2 pillars that already work** (the "Best PM Software" pillar: 88 clicks, position 9 USA / 1-2 Canada). Do not abandon them, they hold authority.
- **Ramp up phases 3 and 4 aggressively.** This is where the audit says the real opportunity to move demos lives.
  - Phase 3 targets: "how to choose property management software", "leasing automation ROI", "centralized leasing benefits".
  - Phase 4 targets: "best AI leasing platform for multifamily", "Yardi integration leasing automation", "[competitor] vs Leasey", "alternatives to [competitor]".
- **Map every new blog to a phase.** The calendar-planner should not let the calendar drift back to all-phase-1-2 content. Aim for a deliberate mix with a phase 3-4 bias in the decision-stage blocks.

## How this maps to our 4 content clusters

- **Modern leasing funnel** spans phases 2-4 (how-to + feature + comparison).
- **AI in property management** is the strongest phase 3-4 cluster (buyer framework, comparisons, "ai property management software" sits at position 45, below page 2, pure opportunity).
- **Operating a Canadian rental business** spans phases 1-3 (market context + Canadian-first decision factors).
- **Inside Leasey** is mostly phase 5 (case studies, founder POV, proof).

## Category queries below page 2 (phase 3-4 opportunity, quantified)

- "ai property management software": position 45 (page 5). Needs a dedicated cornerstone.
- "automated leasing": position 26 (page 3). Our own category language.

These two confirm the leasing-automation cluster has no traction yet and is the clearest phase 3-4 opening.
