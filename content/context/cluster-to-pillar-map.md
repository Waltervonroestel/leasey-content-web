# Cluster ↔ Pillar map — connecting published content to the new editorial line

How the **14 SEO clusters that already exist on leasey.ai** (Alejandra's audit + the GSC-verified counts from `content-audit.md`) connect to the **6 positioning pillars** that drive the new editorial line (`positioning.md`). P4 (Canadian-first) and P6 (US-ready, operator-first) are the dual-market pair: P4 = uncontested niche, P6 = volume market.

Walter uses this to: (a) decide which old pages get re-optimised to reinforce a pillar, (b) spot pages that exist but reinforce no pillar (candidates to deprecate, consolidate, or re-purpose), and (c) see which pillars are under-supported by published content so the calendar prioritises them.

Last refresh: 2026-06-26 | Source counts: GSC 90d (`content-audit.md`).

## Pillar coverage (read across the columns)

### Pillar 1: Speed wins in a cooling market
- **Core clusters carrying it:** Facebook Marketplace, News Pages, Leasing Automation.
- **Partial:** Best PM Software, Vancouver/Canadian markets, Listing Guides, Product/Feature, Comparison, Insights, Benefits.
- **Health:** strong on Marketplace and News (market-data), thin on the core (Leasing Automation cluster is 25 URLs/15 clicks — "dead while being the core"). Every new market-data piece reinforces this pillar.
- **Calendar implication:** keep feeding News Pages with fresh CMHC / Yardi / CBRE pieces; do NOT scale back Marketplace; the cornerstone for Leasing Automation (Aug 6) is the missing piece.

### Pillar 2: An agent, not a widget
- **Core clusters carrying it:** Leasing Automation, Comparison pages, Product/Feature.
- **Partial:** Best PM Software, Tenant Screening, Research, Insights, Marketplace, News, Benefits.
- **Health:** the cluster that most directly carries this (Leasing Automation) is the weakest. Comparison pages exist (18) but have low CTR. The differentiation story has the wrong real estate.
- **Calendar implication:** the highest-leverage move is the Leasing Automation cornerstone + 4-5 honest comparisons in the house format. Aug 6 cornerstone starts that.

### Pillar 3: All-in-one, from listing to signature
- **Core clusters carrying it:** Facebook Marketplace, Best PM Software, Listing Guides, Product/Feature, Comparison, Leasing Automation.
- **Partial:** Tenant Screening, Benefits.
- **Health:** well distributed when the clusters fire. The problem is Best PM Software (5 URLs, 94 clicks, **0.09% CTR with 102K impressions** — the most bleeding cluster on the site) and Product/Feature (only 4 URLs detected).
- **Calendar implication:** the Best PM Software piece (Jul 9) lifts this pillar AND fixes the most bleeding cluster. Then expand Product/Feature pages.

### Pillar 4: Canadian-first
- **Core clusters carrying it:** Vancouver/Canadian markets, Lease Takeover.
- **Partial:** Marketplace, Best PM Software, Tenant Screening, Legal/Lease, Listing Guides, News, Product/Feature, Comparison, Insights, Leasing Automation.
- **Health:** the cluster fully dedicated to Canadian is small (37 URLs) but the pillar bleeds into many other clusters via Toronto/Vancouver/CMHC references. Healthy and well-supported. This is the differentiator and it shows.
- **Calendar implication:** keep the rhythm (one Canadian-anchored piece per week is enough). Do not overcrowd; the pillar already has gravity.

### Pillar 5: Compliance-aware AI
- **Core clusters carrying it:** Legal/Lease Clauses, Tenant Screening, Research.
- **Partial:** Tools/Calculators, Operations & PM Knowledge, Lease Takeover, Comparison, Product/Feature, Leasing Automation, Benefits.
- **Health:** Research (22 URLs, AEO-positioned) is the right home for the heavy compliance content. Legal/Lease (40 URLs, 131 clicks) and Tenant Screening (32 URLs, 71 clicks) carry the operator-facing version.
- **Calendar implication:** the Fair Housing + DOJ piece (Aug 13) is the US version; the Research cluster needs one or two new AEO-cited Canadian pieces (RTA/RTB + fair housing) to balance it.

### Pillar 6: US-ready, operator-first (Sun Belt and secondary markets)
- **Core clusters carrying it:** Facebook Marketplace, News Pages.
- **Partial:** Best PM Software, Tenant Screening, Listing Guides, Product/Feature, Comparison, Leasing Automation.
- **Health:** the pillar is real but **structurally under-supported in published content**. P4 has 37 dedicated URLs (Vancouver/Canadian markets); P6 has no dedicated cluster on the site (US Sun Belt/secondary metros do not have a parallel cluster to the Canadian-cities cluster). The cluster that should carry it most heavily is Listing Guides (which Alejandra noted only converts on Toronto), and most of the US listing guides are weak.
- **Calendar implication:** the 4 US pieces already in the calendar (Jul 16 FB Marketplace US, Aug 13 Fair Housing + DOJ, Sep 1 Sun Belt lease-up, plus the Sep 2-4 LinkedIn + Reddit trio) are the start of building P6 into the published content. To get to parity with P4 over 6-9 months, the calendar needs:
  - 4-5 US-metro listing guides (Austin, Phoenix, Tampa, Charlotte, Dallas) of the same depth as the Toronto one.
  - 1-2 US-Sun-Belt news pieces per quarter (CBRE/Yardi/RealPage anchored).
  - 1 US fair-housing piece per quarter (extends Aug 13 into the Research cluster).
  - The cornerstone for Leasing Automation (Aug 6) is dual-market by design and reinforces P6 alongside P1/P2/P3.
- **Important rule (from `us-market.md`):** never mix CA and US data in the same piece. P4 pieces use CMHC/StatCan/RTA-RTB; P6 pieces use CBRE/Yardi/RealPage/AppFolio/TransUnion/DOJ/Rently. Each piece picks one market and stays there.

To bring P6 to parity with P4 over 6-9 months: 4-5 US-metro listing guides (Austin/Phoenix/Tampa/Charlotte/Dallas), 1-2 US Sun Belt news pieces per quarter, 1 US fair-housing Research piece per quarter.
