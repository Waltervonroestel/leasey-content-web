Source: "Audit report for Leasey" (Coda), Competitive Analysis section. This is the canonical competitor map for the content system. Writer and planner agents use it for comparison content, positioning, and to avoid claims that do not hold up against a real competitor.

The play is to appear ALONGSIDE them on the specific decision-stage keywords (best leasing automation software, AI leasing platform, alternatives to [competitor]). **The buyer comparing options lands on Capterra, G2, or competitors' own comparison pages before reaching Leasey.** That is the gap our comparison content closes.

## What no competitor covers (our open differentiators)

1. **Founder voice + Canadian niche.** Almost all competitors post as a generic corporate brand. Juan and Carlos's personal voice, anchored in real Canadian scenarios (TEREZ, Goldwynn) and the Canadian-first angle, is uncontested.
2. **Facebook Marketplace depth.** No competitor covers this cluster with our depth. It is our biggest cluster by clicks.
3. **/llm-info/ for AEO.** We already have it. Only Entrata shows visible AEO practice (the footer buttons). The sector is starting to move here, we are early.

## Content gaps the audit flags (competitors win, we are absent)

- **Decision-stage / comparison content.** Competitors win the band closest to purchase. Topical authority is already ours; the buyer just lands on competitors first.
- **ROI / benchmark content.** Entrata (Forrester 141% ROI), Buildium (2026 Industry Report), AppFolio (2026 Benchmark Report). We have TEREZ to anchor a real ROI piece.
- **Owned formats we lack:** podcast (only Funnel has one), recurring webinar hub (Entrata, EliseAI), own events (AppFolio FUTURE, Yardi YASC, Entrata Summit, Funnel The Forum), YouTube. Future roadmap, not Q3.

## What to NOT claim against these competitors

- Do not claim Leasey is a better full PMS than AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi, or Entrata. We are a leasing layer that sits on top of the PMS, not a replacement.
- Do not claim Leasey is a better enterprise voice autoresponder than EliseAI.
- Do not claim Leasey is a better self-tour / hardware product than ShowMojo.
- Do not claim Leasey is a better marketing-attribution CRM than Funnel Leasing.
- Honest comparison only. The "where each wins / what we will not claim" structure is the house format and it is what builds trust + earns AI citations.
