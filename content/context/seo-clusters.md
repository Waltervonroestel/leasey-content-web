## What this tells the content system

1. **Facebook Marketplace is the cluster to keep feeding.** Biggest by clicks, functional long-tail. Our calendar already leans here via the FB Marketplace + syndication launches. ✓
2. **Comparison pages have very low CTR but are the decision-stage play.** 19 URLs at position 7-15 with near-zero CTR. The content exists, it is short and weak. The audit says comparison is the highest-return bet. Our job: write strong, honest, long comparisons (the house format). Alejandra's job: title/meta rewrites to lift CTR.
3. **"Leasing Automation" is dead while being the core of the business.** ~25 URLs, ~9 clicks, no keywords. This is the clearest content opportunity: build the cornerstone "automated leasing" / "ai property management software" content (both below page 2). Maps to our AI cluster.
4. **Research pages are AEO assets, not traffic assets.** Measure them in LLM citations, not clicks. Keep writing to be cited.
5. **Tools/Calculators convert best per URL.** Lead-magnet opportunity: package them as downloadables (see strategy notes).

## Cross-cutting findings (context, mostly Alejandra's lane)

- 797 URLs in GSC; only 281 (35%) got >=1 click in 90 days. 518 (65%) got zero. 91 URLs have 500+ impressions and zero CTR ("zombie pages": ranking, not capturing). These are CTR/title fixes, Alejandra's lane.
- Homepage alone = 846 clicks, more than all clusters combined. Strong brand traffic.
- "Best PM Software - 4 popular options": 85K impressions, 0.10% CTR. A title/meta rewrite to 1% CTR unlocks ~800 clicks/quarter without touching content. Alejandra's lane, but it confirms the site has a CAPTURE problem, not a demand problem.
- Cannibalization (multiple FB Marketplace landings, PM Fees, Lease Takeover, Vancouver) = Alejandra's consolidation work, not content.

## Freshness (shared with our editor-qa rule)

PropTech content ages fast (regulation by province/state, speed of the AI category). Audit priority to re-check:
- Articles before the WordPress migration (Jul 2023).
- Listing guides with old data in the slug ("2023-vancouver-2bed-rent-insights").
- Repressed 2025 announcements with features that have since changed.
- Pre-2024 market statistics in research pages.
- Comparisons mentioning competitor features that have been surpassed.

Our editor-qa freshness gating (>90d stale, >30d warning) already enforces this for new content. For existing pages it is Alejandra's audit.

- **Alejandra (technical):** CTR title/meta rewrites, canonicals, 301 redirects, schema (Review, Person, Organization fix), cannibalization consolidation, zombie-page fixes, /a-price/ rename, broken/lost links, indexing.
