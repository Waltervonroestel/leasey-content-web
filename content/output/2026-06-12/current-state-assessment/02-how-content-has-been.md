# 2 - How content has been

This is the diagnostic: what exists, in what voice, on what cadence, and what has actually worked.

## 2.1 Three voice eras still live on the site

### Era 1 - Playful 2023 (En Algun Lugar Estudio)

Where it lives today: older posts at www.leasey.ai/blog/ (Tenant Taming, Unleash Your Inner Wizard, Tea on Student Rentals), all 12 posts at blog.leasey.ai/.

The 2023 brand voice document says: "Blogs should be written in a fun, conversational, casual tone. Get goofy, use metaphors, and toss a meme in there every once in a while."

The ICP that voice was written for: "male, 40+, not tech savvy, lazy, disorganised, managing 100-300 properties."

That is a different ICP than the enterprise / mid-market multifamily we serve today. The Onboarding Guide is explicit: do not replicate this era. But the posts are still indexed and still set tone expectations for new readers landing from search.

### Era 2 - Current B2B operator-facing (the live voice)

Where it lives: the recent posts on www.leasey.ai/blog/ (Property Managers' Complete Guide to Facebook Marketplace, The Future of Your Rental Business, FAQs About Property Management Software).

Voice characteristics, sampled verbatim from the live blog:

- Second person ("you"), addressing the property manager directly
- Question or pain hook in the first two lines ("Still posting Facebook Marketplace listings one by one?")
- Problem-solution framing from line one
- Professional but approachable, light metaphors occasionally
- Numbered steps, comparison tables, scannable structure
- Leasey woven in mid-article, not just at the bottom
- "Schedule a call with Leasey.AI" is the house CTA phrasing

This is the active voice for blog, press release, and the LinkedIn company page. The template is repeatable.

### Era 3 - Founder voices on LinkedIn

From the Onboarding Guide:

- **Carlos**: direct opener, max 3 sentences per paragraph, founder-authentic, never "Excited to announce", bilingual Colombia/Canada context referenced naturally.
- **Juan**: technical accuracy, ~60 words per post, leads with product milestones not relationship check-ins.

Both voices are documented but barely exercised in 2026.

### Three patterns to keep in mind

1. **Facebook Marketplace content is our biggest cluster.** Three of the top ten pages. The repressed announcement on direct syndication slots cleanly into this cluster.
2. **The free tools convert.** Two calculators in the top ten. Tools are content too, and they are working.
3. **Long-form comparison content outperforms everything else by impression volume.** The "Best PMS 4 popular options" article has 83K impressions over 90 days — almost as much as every free tool combined.

## 2.5 What has not worked

- **blog.leasey.ai** generated 39 clicks across 12 renter-focused city guides in 90 days. That subdomain is effectively dormant.
- **The LinkedIn channels** (company page, Carlos, Juan) have produced near zero in the same window.
- **r/LeaseyAI** has not had a single official post yet.
- **No press release has gone out** despite nine repressed announcements waiting.

## 2.6 What I take away from "how it has been"

1. We have a working voice template (Era 2 operator-facing). We do not need to invent it; we need to apply it consistently.
2. We have a publishing problem, not a content problem. The content debt is concentrated and well-defined.
3. The Facebook Marketplace cluster is a topical authority we should keep building on; it is already converting.
4. blog.leasey.ai is at a decision point that founders need to make.
5. The founder profiles are the most underused asset by an order of magnitude.
