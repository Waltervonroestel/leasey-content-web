# Direct Facebook Marketplace syndication: stop posting one listing at a time

Still posting your Facebook Marketplace listings one by one at 11pm? You are not alone, and the math is brutal.

Facebook Marketplace has quietly become one of the highest intent inbound channels in rental, especially for mid-market multifamily and SFR portfolios. The problem is not finding leads there. The problem is volume, timing, and the manual workflow that grinds your team down: post each listing by hand, refresh it before it expires, juggle three messages in five minutes at midnight, then copy and paste replies into your PMS in the morning.

According to ShowMojo, around 70% of rental prospects search after business hours. That is a structural mismatch with how most leasing teams operate. Your agents post during the day, and the inquiries arrive at night.

Today we are rolling out direct Facebook Marketplace syndication inside Leasey.AI. Here is what changes for you.

## What direct syndication actually means

When you list a vacant unit in Leasey, the same listing goes live on Facebook Marketplace automatically. Same photos, same description, same availability. You publish once and Leasey keeps it in sync.

Three concrete benefits for an operator running 100+ doors:

1. **One listing surface.** You stop maintaining a separate "Facebook copy" of your inventory. The source of truth is the unit record in Leasey, and Marketplace reflects it.
2. **Refresh automation.** Facebook Marketplace listings age out fast. Leasey re-publishes on the cadence the platform rewards, so your inventory stays visible without manual reposts.
3. **Lead capture in one inbox.** Inquiries land in your centralised messaging, not in a personal Messenger account that your weekend coverage cannot reach.

## The after-hours problem solved at the source

Facebook Marketplace is one of those channels where speed and time of day make or break conversion. The first reply wins, and the first reply at 11:47pm is almost never a human.

This is where Liza, our 24/7 AI leasing agent, fits naturally with the syndication. When a Marketplace lead comes in at midnight, Liza answers, qualifies against the criteria you set (pets, income, move-in date, lease length), and books a showing on the calendar your team will see in the morning. Operators like Apartment Hub use this combination to keep inquiry response rates close to 100%, compared with the 30 to 40% range typical for manually handled inbound.

## What you stop doing

- Logging into a personal Facebook account to manage business inquiries.
- Copy-pasting replies between Messenger and your CRM.
- Reposting listings every few days to keep them visible.
- Handing off night and weekend coverage to whoever is willing.
