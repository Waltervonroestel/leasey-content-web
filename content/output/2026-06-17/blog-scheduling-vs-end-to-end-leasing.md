# Scheduling alone vs end-to-end leasing: where the leaks really happen

If you only automate the showing scheduler, what happens to everything before and after?

That is the trade-off mid-market property managers face as the AI leasing category matures. According to Frontdesk Research's 2026 State of Multifamily AI report, 94% of operators are adopting AI in some part of their funnel, and the operators who lifted conversion the most (+45% lead-to-lease) were running full AI leasing agents, not point tools. The gap between "automate one step" and "automate the funnel" is widening.

Here is what falls outside a scheduling-only tool, and what you usually rebuild manually around it.

## What scheduling tools handle well

Booking the tour after a qualified lead reaches the calendar. That is real value. It removes a daily annoyance.

## What scheduling tools do not handle

1. **Syndication.** Your listings still go up by hand on Zillow, Kijiji, Facebook Marketplace, and the other 45+ platforms. Leasey publishes once to [48+ marketplaces](/features/marketplace-syndication/).
2. **Lead capture and qualification.** Inquiries from those listings still hit your inbox unsorted. [Liza](/features/liza/), our 24/7 phone and chat agent, answers, qualifies against your criteria, and books the showing automatically. Per Frontdesk Research 2026, full agents respond in 2 to 4 minutes.
3. **Screening and applications.** Credit, background, ID, income, and inconsistency detection happen elsewhere in your stack, then you copy data back. Leasey runs screening with biometric ID and Discrepancy AI inside the same workflow.
4. **Documents and signature.** Lease drafting and signing usually live in a third tool with its own subscription. Leasey auto-fills documents with smart fields and signs natively.

Each handoff between tools is a leak: data re-entered by hand, leads dropped on the seams, your team chasing context across four browser tabs.

## The integration barrier is the real story

Per a Propmodo / Frontdesk Research review of multifamily PropTech in 2026, integration with existing systems is the #1 adoption barrier, above cost, ROI, and training. Operators do not want to switch PMS, and they do not want a stack of seven tools either. They want one layer that sits on top.

That is the position operators like Three Shores landed in. They kept their existing PMS, plugged Leasey.AI on top via two-way sync, and gave their team one place for syndication, lead handling, showings, screening, and lease signature. The category they chose was not a better scheduler. It was end-to-end leasing.
