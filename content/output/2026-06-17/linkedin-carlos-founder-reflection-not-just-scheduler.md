When we started building Leasey.AI, the easy path was to build a better scheduler.

That was the category venture money was funding, and the market wanted it. We had seen the pain at institutional operators like QuadReal and Aquilini, where a missed showing could cost a building tens of thousands in a soft month. A clean booking flow alone would have been a real product.

We chose differently, and the data backs why.

Per Propmodo's 2026 PropTech review, integration with existing systems is now the #1 adoption barrier in multifamily leasing, above cost and ROI. When we sat in operator offices, that showed up as: the lead never reached the scheduler, or screening took five days after the showing, or the lease lived in a third tool nobody owned. Automating one step inside a broken chain felt like polishing the door of a leaking building.

So we built the chain. Listing to Liza to showing to screening to signature, in one layer. Operators like Three Shores keep their PMS and stop stitching the rest of the funnel together by hand.

If we had built only the scheduler, we would have a tidier product. Easier to demo. But we would not be solving what was actually slowing down the leasing teams I grew up watching in Canada.

If you are evaluating leasing automation and feel pulled toward the easier category, this is the trade-off worth thinking through: https://www.leasey.ai/get-started/
