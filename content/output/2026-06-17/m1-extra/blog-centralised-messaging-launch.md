# Centralised messaging: every lead conversation in one thread

How many windows does your leasing team need open before they can reply to a prospect?

For most mid-market property management operations, the honest answer is four to six. Facebook Marketplace Messenger. Zillow's internal portal. Kijiji's inbox. Email. Maybe a CRM that ingests some of it. Maybe a personal cell phone that gets the late-night texts. Each lead arrives on a different surface, with different reply latency and different context for the agent.

That fragmentation is where leads die. Not from refusal, from friction.

Today we are rolling out centralised messaging inside Leasey.AI. Here is what changes.

## The new normal: one thread per prospect, regardless of channel

When a prospect inquires from Facebook Marketplace, Zillow, Kijiji, or email, the inbound message lands in the same thread inside Leasey. The original platform is tagged so your team knows the source, but the conversation lives in one place. Liza, our 24/7 AI leasing agent, can answer the inquiry, qualify against the criteria you set, and book the showing from the same thread, on any source.

For your team, the practical change:

1. **One inbox to clear.** No more switching tabs to track the same prospect's three follow-ups across three platforms.
2. **One conversation history per prospect.** The third message from a Marketplace lead inherits the context of the first two, even if Liza handled them while the team was off.
3. **One handoff between Liza and the human.** When a thread requires a human, the agent picks up where Liza left off, with the full conversation visible and the qualification context attached.

## Why this matters in the soft-market math

According to ShowMojo, around 70% of rental prospects search after business hours. Per the 2026 Propmodo industry review of multifamily PropTech, integration with existing systems is now the #1 adoption barrier in the category. Both findings push the same conclusion: the leasing team that wins the cooling-market quarter is the one that handles inbound across channels without dropping context or losing time of day.

For operators like Three Shores, a centralised inbox means the team no longer reconciles screenshots between platforms at end-of-week. The dashboard shows what is in the funnel, by channel, by stage, in real time.

## What this does not do

Centralised messaging does not replace the leasing manager. It removes the channel-by-channel friction so the manager can spend the day on the conversations that move the lease, not on the busywork that surrounds them. The 20% of prospect questions that require judgement still land with a human, with full context.
