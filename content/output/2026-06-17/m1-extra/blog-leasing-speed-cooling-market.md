# What to do when vacancy rises and rents fall: the lever you do control

You cannot control the macro market. You can control the lead that arrived at 11pm.

According to Yardi's Q1 2026 Multifamily Report, Canadian national vacancy is at 5.1%, the ninth straight quarterly rise. Calgary 7.3%, Edmonton 6.2%. Annual rent growth has slowed to 2.7%, a four-year low, and lease-over-lease on new leases is down 1.0% nationally. For property managers operating in the Prairie corridor or any softening US market, the conventional levers all point inward: pricing, concessions, listing quality, marketing spend.

Those are real. But they fight a market trend, not a process trend. There is one lever that scales with your operation and not with the market: the speed at which you respond to inquiries.

## Why response speed is the underrated lever

According to ShowMojo, around 70% of rental prospects search after business hours. That is a structural mismatch with how most leasing teams operate. Your agents post during the day. The inquiries arrive at night.

Industry response rates for inbound rental inquiries hover around 30 to 40% on manual workflows. The other 60 to 70% are leads that never got an answer fast enough to matter. In a soft market, those are the leads your competitor will close.

## Three things this changes inside the funnel

1. **You stop losing inquiries to time of day.** The Calgary lease-up that fills 90% in summer can fill the last 10% in shoulder season only if the inquiries at 11pm get answered before morning.
2. **You stop losing leads to channel friction.** Facebook Marketplace, Zillow, Kijiji, and Apartments.com surface inquiries at different times and in different formats. A centralised intake (one inbox, one qualification logic) cuts the leakage between channels.
3. **You free the agents for the work that needs a human.** Per a 2026 Propmodo industry review, around 80% of prospect questions before booking fall into a finite set of scenarios AI handles well. The 20% that need judgement still go to the human team.

## What this looks like operationally

For TEREZ, a 154-unit lease-up in Cochrane just outside Calgary, the cooling corridor is real. Vacancy in the adjacent market sits at 7.3%. Their team needed a way to keep response rate near 100% without adding leasing headcount through the soft season.

The market is what it is. The lead at 11pm is the part you can do something about.
