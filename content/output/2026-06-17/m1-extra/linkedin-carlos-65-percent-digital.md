A statistic worth sitting with: 65% of residents prefer digital leasing methods, per Propmodo's 2026 PropTech review.

That number reframes what "good service" looks like in 2026. The resident who toured at 11pm because they could text a chatbot to schedule the appointment is now the rule, not the exception. The team that requires a 9am phone call to book a tour is the one losing the lease.

I bring this up because I still get asked, "Carlos, is this AI thing maybe a fad?" Hard to call it a fad when more than half of the people doing the renting say they prefer it.

For operators like Goldwynn Residences, that resident preference shows up in the conversion data. The case study we published last quarter has the team running a leasing experience where the prospect controls the pace: chat now, ask the question now, book the showing now.

Our job as operators is not to debate whether AI belongs in leasing. It is to choose AI that actually closes the loop, not a widget that captures a form.

If you are evaluating where to start, the question to ask is whether the prospect can complete the entire path to a booked tour without a human on the other end. https://www.leasey.ai/get-started/
