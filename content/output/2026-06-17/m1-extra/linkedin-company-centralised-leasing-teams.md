A pattern we keep seeing across large operators: the on-site leasing agent is becoming a centralised role. One team, multiple properties, real-time visibility across the portfolio.

The logic is straightforward. With vacancy climbing in major Canadian markets (Yardi Q1 2026: 5.1% national, ninth straight quarterly rise), the cost of a slow lead handoff multiplies. A centralised team can run shared SLAs, shared scripts, and shared screening criteria, instead of letting each property reinvent the funnel.

What this requires is not "more software". It is one layer that owns the funnel: listings, inquiries, qualification, scheduling, screening, and lease signature, with visibility into every step. The PMS keeps the units. The leasing layer keeps the funnel moving.
