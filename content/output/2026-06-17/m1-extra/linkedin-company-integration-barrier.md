Integration with existing systems is now the #1 adoption barrier in multifamily PropTech, above cost, above ROI, above training. That is the conclusion from the 2026 Propmodo / Frontdesk Research review.

It tracks with what we hear from operators every week. Nobody wants to switch their PMS. Nobody wants a stack of seven tools that need to be reconciled by hand at month-end. The question on the table is: can a new layer sit on top of what we already run, or do we have to rip out something that works.

Leasey.AI is built for the "sit on top" answer. Two-way sync with Yardi, integrations with Buildium and Rent Manager, and a standalone mode for operators without a PMS. Implementation in a day.

That position is why operators like Three Shores chose Leasey instead of replacing their stack. They kept the PMS, plugged Leasey on top, and got one place for syndication, lead handling, showings, screening, and lease signature.
