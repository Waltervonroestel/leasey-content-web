SingleKey screening is now live inside Leasey.AI for Canadian operators. Credit, identity, eviction history, and Canadian-specific data points run inside the same workflow that handles inquiry, qualification, showing, and lease.

The Canadian leasing stack has always been an awkward fit for US-first PropTech. Provincial regulation, Canadian-only platforms (Kijiji, Duuo, now SingleKey), and screening criteria that do not map cleanly to US bureaus. Most leasing tools treat the country as a US footnote and the operator ends up duct-taping the screening step.

That is the friction this integration removes. For TEREZ in Cochrane and other Canadian operators on the platform, the screening decision now lives inside the leasing funnel, with the same audit trail as every other step.

The 48+ marketplaces, Liza, Smart Leases, and now SingleKey screening operate as one layer. No extra subscription, no extra login.

Canadian property managers, schedule a call with our team: https://www.leasey.ai/get-started/
