# Leasey.AI Integrates SingleKey for Canadian Tenant Screening

## Combined platform brings end-to-end leasing automation and Canadian-built screening into one workflow for property managers

NEW YORK, NY / VANCOUVER, BC, June 30, 2026. Leasey.AI, the leasing automation platform for property managers in Canada and the United States, today announced a partnership with SingleKey, the Canadian tenant screening company. The integration brings credit, identity, and Canadian-specific tenant data into the Leasey.AI workflow alongside listing syndication, AI lead handling, showing scheduling, and lease signature.

The announcement comes as Canadian property managers face an uneven market. Per Yardi's Q1 2026 Multifamily Report, national vacancy is at 5.1%, with Calgary at 7.3% and Edmonton at 6.2%, while Halifax sits at 2.8%. Provincial regulation, Canadian-only platforms, and screening requirements that do not map cleanly to United States bureaus have historically forced Canadian operators to assemble their leasing stack piece by piece. The SingleKey integration is part of Leasey.AI's Canadian-first strategy, alongside existing integrations with Kijiji and Duuo by Co-operators.

For operators like TEREZ, a 154-unit lease-up in Cochrane, Alberta, the SingleKey integration means tenant screening now happens inside the same funnel that captured the inquiry and booked the showing. Credit, identity, and eviction history checks run automatically once an application is submitted, and the data flows back into the leasing dashboard with a full audit trail.

"Canadian property managers have been waiting for a leasing layer that treats their market as a first-class operating environment, not a United States footnote," said Juan Leal, CEO and CPO of Leasey.AI. "The SingleKey integration is the screening piece of that promise. The next pieces are already on the roadmap."

## About Leasey.AI

Leasey.AI is an AI-powered leasing automation platform for property managers in Canada and the United States. The platform combines listing syndication to 48+ marketplaces, Liza (a 24/7 AI leasing agent on phone and chat), automated showing coordination, tenant screening, and native lease signature. Founded in 2023 by brothers Juan and Carlos Leal and operated by Silver Homes Technology Inc., Leasey.AI is backed by DMZ Ventures and headquartered in Canada, with offices in New York, Vancouver, and Toronto.
