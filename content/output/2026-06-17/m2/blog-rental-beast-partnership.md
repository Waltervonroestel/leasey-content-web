# Rental Beast partnership: how cross-marketplace syndication actually works for property managers

How many of the marketplaces your team should be on are you actually publishing to?

For the average mid-market property management operation in North America, the honest answer is fewer than half. Manual posting across the major platforms is doable. Adding Rental Beast's network of MLS-connected listing surfaces, multi-market portals, and regional MLS-fed sites pushes the workload past sustainable.

That is the gap the Rental Beast partnership inside Leasey.AI closes. Both companies announced the integration this month. Here is what it changes inside the operator workflow.

## What the Rental Beast integration adds

The 48+ marketplaces Leasey already syndicated to (Zillow, Kijiji, Facebook Marketplace, Apartments.com, Trulia, HotPads, and more) now extend with Rental Beast's MLS-connected network. That brings rental listings into the MLS-fed surfaces real estate professionals check daily.

For the operator running heavy showing volume, that surface area matters. According to Justin Rogers at The Rockingham Group, who runs more than 80 tours per week, the difference between a unit appearing on every relevant platform and missing the right one is the difference between a 7-day vacancy and a 21-day vacancy.

## The workflow change

Inside Leasey, a vacant unit publishes once. The listing flows to the existing 48+ marketplaces and through Rental Beast's network simultaneously. Inquiries land in the same centralised inbox. [Liza](/features/liza/) answers them on phone or chat 24/7. The team sees the conversation, the source, and the status in one dashboard.

The change is not "more places to post." It is "one place to manage all the places to post."

## Why this matters now

Per the 2026 Propmodo / Frontdesk Research review, integration with existing systems is the #1 PropTech adoption barrier. Operators do not want a new tool to publish to MLS-connected listings. They want their current leasing tool to do it. The Rental Beast integration delivers that, with no additional subscription on the Leasey side.

According to ShowMojo, around 70% of rental prospects search after business hours. More marketplaces multiplied by after-hours inquiries equals more leads. Liza catches those after-hours leads in any case. The Rental Beast integration grows the pool.

## What this does not do

Rental Beast inside Leasey.AI is a syndication and lead reach expansion, not a replacement for your MLS membership. Operators who want listing-side MLS participation still need that membership directly.
