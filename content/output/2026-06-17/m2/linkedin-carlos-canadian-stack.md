A reminder that comes up often when I talk to Canadian operators: the US-first PropTech stack does not always translate.

Per Yardi's Q1 2026 Multifamily Report, Canadian markets are not moving in sync. Calgary at 7.3% vacancy, Edmonton at 6.2%, Halifax at 2.8%. Provincial regulation, screening platforms, listing portals, and tenant insurance providers operate differently across the country.

For the leasing tool the team adopts, that asymmetry matters. Most platforms designed in the United States treat Canada as a configuration toggle. The ones that get adopted treat it as a first-class operating environment.

We built Leasey.AI Canadian-first for that reason. Kijiji syndication, Duuo by Co-operators for insurance, SingleKey for tenant screening (live this week). The platform speaks the languages, references the regulations, and integrates with the screening systems your team already trusts.

For an operator like TEREZ in Cochrane, the integration list is not a marketing detail. It is the difference between adopting one platform and stitching three.
