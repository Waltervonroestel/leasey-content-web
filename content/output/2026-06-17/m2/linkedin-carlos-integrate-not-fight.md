We had a conversation early in Leasey's life where the easy product to build was the PMS killer.

Replace the incumbent. New ledger. New tenant accounts. New everything. Investors wanted it. Some operators wanted it. We had the engineering to try.

We chose differently for one specific reason. The teams we sold to had spent ten years configuring Yardi or Buildium to their workflows. Ripping that out for a leasing problem felt like asking them to rebuild the house to replace one window.

Per the 2026 Propmodo PropTech review, integration is now the #1 PropTech adoption barrier, above cost, above ROI, above training. That number says the same thing the operators said two years ago: "do not make me switch."

So we built Leasey.AI as the leasing layer that sits on top. Two-way sync with Yardi. Buildium. Rent Manager. Or standalone if there is no PMS. Operators like Goldwynn kept their stack and added the leasing piece, in days, not quarters.

The product I would have built three years ago would have been more elegant. It would also have been less used. Sometimes the right answer is not the cleaner architecture. It is the one the team can actually adopt.

If you are evaluating how to add leasing automation without replacing your stack, that is the conversation: https://www.leasey.ai/get-started/
