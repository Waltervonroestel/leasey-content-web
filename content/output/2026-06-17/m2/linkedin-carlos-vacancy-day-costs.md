Every day of vacancy on a 154-unit lease-up is a margin point you do not get back.

I think about this often because of TEREZ, the Cochrane lease-up our team has been supporting through Q1 and Q2. Calgary vacancy is at 7.3% per Yardi's Q1 2026 report, the highest of the major Canadian markets. Cochrane sits in that gravity field.

The math from the operator side. A unit at $1,800 a month that takes an extra 30 days to lease costs the building $60 per day. Across 20 units in motion, that is $1,200 a day. Across a full quarter of a lease-up, that is real money. And the conversation in the budget meeting is never "we had a slow vacancy quarter." It is "we lost 4% on our pro forma."

So when we talk to operators about leasing automation, the framing that lands is not "save time." It is "the prospect at 11pm who never got a response is a margin point you do not get back."
