The Canadian leasing stack is quietly consolidating around four pillars:

1. Listing distribution: Kijiji, MLS, Zillow Canada
2. Tenant insurance: Duuo by Co-operators
3. Tenant screening: SingleKey
4. Leasing automation: Leasey.AI (now live with SingleKey)

This is the version most mid-market Canadian operators are landing on as 2026 progresses. Each pillar is built for Canadian regulation, currency, ID norms, and provincial tenancy rules.

Per Yardi's Q1 2026 Multifamily Report, the Canadian rental market is increasingly uneven (Calgary 7.3%, Halifax 2.8%, national average 5.1% and rising). That asymmetry rewards operators with platforms that respect provincial differences instead of papering over them.

The SingleKey integration that went live last month is part of that posture. For TEREZ in Cochrane, credit, identity, eviction history, and Canadian-specific tenant data flow into the same Leasey leasing workflow that handles inquiry, qualification, showing, and lease signature.
