A pattern worth checking in your AI leasing contract before you scale: where does the indemnification language put the compliance burden if a tenant sues over a screening decision?

For most contracts in this category, the answer today is "with the operator." The vendor's terms protect the vendor's product. Your team carries the legal exposure for how the product is used inside your funnel.

Given the trajectory of DOJ vs RealPage, the addition of Greystar as co-defendant in 2025, and Harbor Group's separate scrutiny over voucher screening (per Multifamily Dive and Law360), that is a position worth negotiating, not accepting as standard.

Before signing or renewing, ask:

1. Who is named on the indemnification clause if a screening decision is challenged?
2. Does the vendor agree to retain decision logs for the period your jurisdiction requires?
3. Will the vendor's compliance team participate in an audit or testify if needed?
