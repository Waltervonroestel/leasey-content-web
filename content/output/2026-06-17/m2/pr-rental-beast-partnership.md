# Leasey.AI Joins Rental Beast Network for Cross-Marketplace Listing Reach

## Integration extends Leasey.AI's syndication from 48+ marketplaces into MLS-connected rental surfaces, with single-publish workflow for property managers

NEW YORK, NY / VANCOUVER, BC, July 30, 2026. Leasey.AI, the leasing automation platform for property managers in Canada and the United States, today announced a partnership with Rental Beast, the rental network connecting MLS-fed listing surfaces across North America. The integration extends Leasey.AI's existing syndication network of 48+ marketplaces into the MLS-connected channels that real estate professionals check daily.

The announcement comes as North American rental markets reward speed and reach at the listing step. Per Yardi's Q1 2026 Multifamily Report, Canadian national vacancy is at 5.1%, with significant variation by market. Operators that publish to more listing surfaces and respond to inquiries faster lease vacant units in fewer days. The integration with Rental Beast lowers the operational cost of expanding listing reach.

For operators like The Rockingham Group, which runs more than 80 tours per week across its portfolio, the integration removes the manual step of pushing listings to MLS-fed channels separately from the operator's existing syndication workflow. A vacant unit posted in Leasey now flows to 48+ marketplaces plus Rental Beast's network in one publish action.

"Operators do not want more tools. They want the tool they already trust to reach more places," said Juan Leal, CEO and CPO of Leasey.AI. "The Rental Beast partnership is the cleanest version of that: same publish step, more surfaces, no extra subscription."

## About Leasey.AI

Leasey.AI is an AI-powered leasing automation platform for property managers in Canada and the United States. The platform combines listing syndication to 48+ marketplaces, Liza (a 24/7 AI leasing agent on phone and chat), automated showing coordination, tenant screening, and native lease signature. Founded in 2023 by brothers Juan and Carlos Leal and operated by Silver Homes Technology Inc., Leasey.AI is backed by DMZ Ventures and headquartered in Canada, with offices in New York, Vancouver, and Toronto.
