- Leasing is the product, not a feature — every part of Leasey.AI exists to move a unit from listing to signed lease faster. In a full suite, leasing shares the roadmap with accounting, maintenance, and reporting.
- 48+ marketplaces from one entry, including Canada — Zillow, Apartments.com, Facebook Marketplace, Kijiji, Realtor.ca, and more in one click. The Canadian channels are first-class, not an afterthought.
- AI agents that carry the whole lead-to-lease flow — Leasey.AI answers every inquiry 24/7, pre-screens, and books showings on its own. Several suites offer AI assistance, but as one module beside everything else.
