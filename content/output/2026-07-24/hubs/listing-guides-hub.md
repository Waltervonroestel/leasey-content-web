1. Prepare the unit — 10 to 20 professional photos and an honest, disclosure-ready description. Leasey.AI's description generator drafts the listing for you.
2. Price to the submarket — benchmark rent against live comparables by neighbourhood, not a citywide average. Leasey.AI's market rent comparison flags over- and under-priced units.
3. Post everywhere at once — Zillow, Apartments.com, Facebook Marketplace, and the regional sites that matter. Leasey.AI syndicates to 48+ marketplaces from one entry.
