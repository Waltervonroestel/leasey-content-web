## SECTION 1 — The 7 tools (variable block, table)
Columns: Tool | What it does | Link
1. Tenant Income Qualification Calculator — checks whether an applicant's income meets your requirement using the 3x rent rule, the 30% rule, or a custom multiplier you set.
2. Security Deposit Calculator — calculates the maximum allowable deposit by location, rent, and property features, including state limits and deposit interest.
3. Rent Increase Timing Calculator — recommends an increase percentage, amount, timing, and effective date from current rent, market rent, tenant quality, and strategy.
4. Vacancy Cost Calculator — shows the true cost of every vacant day from rent, vacancy period, and turnover costs, with lost revenue per day and total cost.
5. 1% Rule Investment Calculator — evaluates whether a property meets the 1% rule and related benchmarks from purchase price and expected rent.
6. Rental Cash Flow Calculator — analyses profitability from purchase price, financing, rent, and operating expenses, returning monthly and annual cash flow.
7. Property Description Generator — generates listing descriptions to get vacant units live across marketplaces faster.
