## Positioning against the reported overlap

The report treats this page as overlapping `/resources/leasing-team-consistency/`. Even setting aside that the other page is unreachable, the two angles were never the same and the copy must not drift between them:

- **This page speaks to the PROSPECT.** The subject is the renter's experience: how fast they get answered, whether the information matches, whether the booking flow feels the same at every building. The reader cares because it shapes the operator's reputation.
- **The other page speaks to the TEAM.** Its subject is agent performance: who is hitting response times, how work is distributed, how managers see it.

Keep every sentence on this landing pointed at the prospect's side of the counter. Agent performance dashboards, individual rep metrics, and team management framing do not belong here even though the underlying product feature is shared.
