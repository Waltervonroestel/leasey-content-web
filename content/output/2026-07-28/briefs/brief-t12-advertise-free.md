## Comparison table structure

Rows are the platforms the page already discusses, plus the ones the top-three name that the page omits. Columns are the decision criteria the four striking-distance queries imply.

**Columns, four:** Platform | Free listing? | Best for | Catch

Audience reach was considered as a fifth column and dropped. Every reach figure available is a third-party traffic estimate that cannot be verified on the platform's own page, so the column could only be filled with numbers this brief is deleting elsewhere.

| Platform | Free listing? | Best for | Catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zillow Rental Manager | Check current terms on Zillow's own pricing page before writing | Single units, broad reach | Pricing terms NOT VERIFIABLE at time of brief. State no figure until confirmed |
| Apartments.com | Check current terms on the platform's own page | Multifamily and apartments | Premium placement is paid. Confirm on source before writing |
| Facebook Marketplace | No listing fee for private listings | Fast local reach, high volume | Highest scam-clone exposure. Links to the scams page |
| Craigslist | Free to post except in three metros | Local, budget-conscious | [$5 per apartment rental post in the Boston, Chicago, and NYC areas](https://www.craigslist.org/about/help/posting_fees). No verification layer |
| Realtor.com | Check current terms on the platform's own page | Single-family homes | Thinner rental audience than Zillow |
| Zumper | Check current terms on the platform's own page | Urban apartments | Upsells promoted listings |
| PadMapper | Check current terms on the platform's own page | Map-first searchers | Pulls from other sources, less control |

**Sourcing rule for this table, and why most cells are empty of figures.** Every pricing claim in the published table must link to that platform's own current pricing page. Only Craigslist could be verified for this brief, and its cell carries the link. `zillow.com` returns HTTP 403 to automated fetching and its domain is blocked to the browser tooling available here, so the earlier "free for the first 30 days" and "charges after 30 days in most markets" claims had no source and have been removed rather than softened. The writer opens each remaining platform's pricing page manually and fills the cell with what that page says, plus the link. Any cell that still cannot be verified ships as "varies by market, see [platform] pricing", never an assumed number, and never a figure carried over from a competitor article.

**Do not put a Leasey.AI row in this table.** The table answers "where do I post for free". Leasey.AI is not a free platform, and inserting it breaks the reader's trust in the comparison. Leasey.AI appears after the table, framed as what you use when posting to all of them by hand stops being viable.

## Writing rules

- **Intro:** first paragraph answers where to post for free, naming the top platforms. Maximum 3 lines. Zero links.
- **Paragraphs:** maximum 4 lines, one idea each, active voice.
- **Bold:** the platform name or the catch a scanner needs. Maximum 2 to 3 per section, never whole sentences.
- **Length:** the 800 to 1,000 word rule is **suspended**. The page holds 56 keywords across 18 headings and the table adds length.

## Guardrails

British English · Oxford comma · zero em-dashes · "Leasey.AI" capitalisation · active voice · no keyword stuffing.
Banned words: streamline, seamless, unlock, empower, robust, transform, game-changer.

Note: platform names keep their own spelling. British English governs our prose, not third-party brand names.
