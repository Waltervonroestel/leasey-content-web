Writing rules

  • Intro: first paragraph answers where to post for free, naming the top platforms. Maximum 3 lines. Zero links.
  • Paragraphs: maximum 4 lines, one idea each, active voice.
  • Bold: the platform name or the catch a scanner needs. Maximum 2 to 3 per section, never whole sentences.
  • Length: the 800 to 1,000 word rule is suspended. The page holds 56 keywords across 18 headings and the table adds length.

Guardrails

British English · Oxford comma · zero em-dashes · "Leasey.AI" capitalisation · active voice · no keyword stuffing.

Banned words: streamline, seamless, unlock, empower, robust, transform, game-changer.

Note: platform names keep their own spelling. British English governs our prose, not third-party brand names.
