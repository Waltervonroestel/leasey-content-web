# Leasey.AI Adds REW.ca to Its Listing Syndication Network

## Canadian operators reach one of the country's established property search audiences from the same single publish action, with no setup and no additional subscription

NEW YORK, NY / VANCOUVER, BC, [DATE]. Leasey.AI, the leasing automation platform for property managers in Canada and the United States, today announced that REW.ca is live as a syndication channel inside the platform. Active listings from Leasey.AI customers publish to REW.ca automatically, with no action required from operators.

The announcement comes as Canadian rental demand shifts underneath operators in exactly the provinces REW.ca serves. Per RBC Economics and StatCan data reported in May 2026, Canada's non-permanent resident population fell from 3.149 million in October 2024 to 2.676 million by January 2026, a drop of more than 470,000. Toronto lost roughly 80,000 residents in 2025 and Vancouver roughly 21,000, with Ontario and British Columbia absorbing the steepest declines. The tenants who used to arrive on their own no longer do, so where a listing appears has started to matter again.

For operators like TEREZ, the 154-unit lease-up in Cochrane, Alberta managed by SunPeak Property Management, adding a channel has historically meant another login, another listing to keep current, and another inbox to watch. With REW.ca inside Leasey.AI, the unit that goes live in the platform reaches the channel on the same publish action, and the inquiries land in the same place Liza already answers.

"Adding a channel should not cost the operator anything. Not a login, not a workflow change, not a subscription," said Juan Leal, CEO and CPO of Leasey.AI. "REW.ca is live on the same publish action as every other channel we carry. Canadian operators reach it without doing anything differently."
