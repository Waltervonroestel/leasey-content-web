## 7. Figures and sources (Rule D)

### Both existing figures are removed

| Current claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "The Smart Route Planner cuts travel distance by **25 to 35%**" | **REMOVE.** Searched `products.md` and all of `context/`: the Smart Route Planner is documented as "optimises showing routes", **with no figures attached**. No internal source, no external source |
| "Agents complete up to **40% more showings** per day with the same staff" | **REMOVE.** Same case. No source anywhere |

**Why this matters more on this page than anywhere else in the cluster.** The sales argument here is "do not hire". A COO reading "40% more showings per agent" does the headcount arithmetic with that number and makes a staffing decision from it. If the figure does not hold, the consequence is not a marketing embarrassment. It is a hiring decision a customer got wrong using our number.

**What replaces them.** Describe capacity by mechanism rather than by outcome. The Smart Route Planner groups showings by geographic proximity and builds the visiting order. That is verifiable, it is genuinely useful, and it promises no multiple.

Searching this intent surfaces attractive figures: 90% of leasing activities automatable, staffing ratios of one agent per 200 to 400 units, $200 to $500 per month against a $35,000 to $55,000 salary. **Several of these trace back to leasey.ai pages.**

Using them would be circular self-citation: we would be sourcing our claim to ourselves and presenting it as independent. That is a blocker, not a caution. It is the same failure that produced the "35% of leads lost" figure removed from the Task 1 brief.

## 10. Writing rules

**Language and typography**
- British English throughout. ⚠️ This page has the heaviest exposure of the three: **"optimized" and "optimization" appear in current headings**, and "inquiry" should be "enquiry". Sweep for **-ize** and **-ization**.
- Oxford comma.
- "Leasey.AI" with that exact capitalisation.
- **Zero em-dashes.** There are 25 today, the worst of the three.

**Brand density**
- Currently 39 mentions in 1,999 words, a density of 2.0%. Target: **under 1%**, roughly 16 to 19 mentions.
- **One "Leasey.AI" per H2 section at most.**

**Tone, specific to this page**
- Automation redirects work; it does not remove people. Block 7 already frames it correctly. Keep that framing throughout, including in the summary paragraph.
- Avoid any phrasing that reads as a redundancy argument. The buyer is growing, not cutting.

**Structure**
- Paragraphs of four lines maximum, one idea each, active voice.
- Bold two or three phrases per H2 at most.
- Intro: three lines maximum, zero links.

**Length**
- Rule 18 does **not** apply. Conversion drives length.

**Banned words:** streamline, seamless, unlock, empower, robust, transform, game-changer.
