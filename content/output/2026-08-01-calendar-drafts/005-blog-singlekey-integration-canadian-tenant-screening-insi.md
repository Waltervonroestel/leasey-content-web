SingleKey integration: Canadian tenant screening inside the leasing funnel, with the report on the same screen as the application

What is the SingleKey integration in Leasey.AI?


The SingleKey integration is a native connection between Leasey.AI's application pipeline and SingleKey's Canadian tenant screening service. When an applicant submits through Leasey.AI, the screening request fires automatically, and the completed report attaches directly to the application file. Your coordinator reviews everything in one place.


Before this integration, the typical mid-market workflow looked like this:


1. Application submitted to Leasey.AI.
2. Coordinator copies tenant name and email into SingleKey separately.
3. Coordinator waits for the report, downloads the PDF.
4. Coordinator opens the application file, attaches the PDF, updates the status manually.


For a team processing 30 to 50 applications per week, that manual handoff adds up to hours of administrative time that produces zero analytical value. The integrated flow eliminates steps two through four entirely.




Why did Leasey.AI choose SingleKey for Canadian screening?


SingleKey is the screening provider purpose-built for Canadian rental operators, and it covers three requirements that US-first screening tools consistently miss.


First, Canadian credit bureau coverage. SingleKey pulls from both Equifax and TransUnion Canada without manual workarounds or US-defaulting logic. Most US-first screening platforms require Canadian operators to request a separate report or use a third-party add-on, which breaks the workflow.


Second, provincial ID verification. Canadian provinces issue their own identification documents, and SingleKey's verification engine handles provincial IDs natively rather than defaulting to US driver's licence formats. This matters for operators in Quebec (where the SAAQ licence differs from other provinces) and for applicants who hold provincial photo ID cards rather than passports.


Third, landlord-contacted rental history. Instead of relying on tenant-submitted references (which are easy to fabricate), SingleKey contacts previous landlords directly. In a market where TransUnion's research on synthetic identity fraud (https://newsroom.transunion.com/transunion-research-highlights-power-of-public-data-in-uncovering-33b-synthetic-identity-threat/) puts synthetic identity fraud exposure at $3.3 billion for the year ending 2024, independent verification of rental history is not optional.


Leasey.AI's positioning pillar here is P4: Canadian-first. Most leasing tools treat Canada as a footnote to their US product. Leasey.AI is built for Canadian regulation, Canadian platforms (Kijiji, Realtor.ca), and Canadian screening from the ground up, with US coverage layered on top rather than the other way around.

Frequently asked questions


Does the SingleKey integration work for US properties?


SingleKey is designed for Canadian credit, identity, and rental history checks. For US properties, Leasey.AI connects with US-based screening providers that pull from US credit bureaus and comply with FCRA requirements. The screening step works the same way inside the platform regardless of jurisdiction.


Do I need a separate SingleKey account to use this integration?


No. The integration is native, meaning the screening request fires from within Leasey.AI using your operator credentials. You do not need to log into SingleKey separately, download reports, or manage a parallel account. Setup takes one onboarding session with your Leasey.AI Customer Success manager.


How long does a SingleKey screening report take to come back?


Most SingleKey reports return within minutes for standard credit and identity checks. Landlord-contacted rental history checks may take longer depending on previous landlord response times. The report attaches to the application file automatically when ready, so your team does not need to check back manually.


Can I still use my existing screening provider alongside SingleKey?


Yes. Leasey.AI does not force a single screening provider. If your portfolio spans both Canadian and US properties, you can use SingleKey for Canadian applicants and a US provider for American applicants, with both reports appearing inside the same application workflow.


What tenant data does SingleKey verify in Canada?


SingleKey verifies Canadian credit history (Equifax and TransUnion Canada), government-issued provincial ID, employment and income, and rental history through direct landlord contact. This covers the core verification needs for Canadian residential leasing without requiring US-centric workarounds.


Is Leasey.AI a property management system?


No. Leasey.AI is a front-office leasing automation and CRM layer that sits on top of existing PMS tools like Yardi, Buildium, or Rent Manager, or runs standalone. It handles the funnel from listing to signed lease; the PMS handles rent collection and active-lease management after the signature.
