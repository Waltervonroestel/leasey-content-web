Leasey.AI and AppFolio: where each one wins, and what we will not claim


A prospect enquires at 21:40 on a Sunday. If nobody answers until Monday morning, that lead has already toured somewhere else. That gap is why the tooling conversation changed this year. 
AppFolio's 2025 Property Management Benchmark Report shows AI adoption in property management rising from 21 percent to 34 percent in a single year. Frontdesk Research's 2026 State of Multifamily AI report separately found that 85 percent of adopters report measurable improvements in lead-to-lease conversion. The market is moving fast, and the question operators ask most often is not "should I use technology?" but "which tool does what, and do I need both?"


If you are comparing Leasey.AI and AppFolio, the first thing to get straight is that they are not the same category of product. Confusing the two is the most common buyer mistake in this comparison. 




What is AppFolio?


AppFolio is a full property management system (PMS) that covers accounting, maintenance requests, owner portals, lease tracking, tenant communications, and a leasing module, all in one platform. It is one of the most established PMS products for mid-market multifamily in North America, with deep functionality across the entire operation, not just leasing.


AppFolio's strength is breadth. If you need a single system of record for everything from rent collection to maintenance dispatch to owner reporting, AppFolio delivers that in one login. Its leasing module handles applications, screening, and lease generation as part of the broader operational suite.




What is Leasey.AI?


Leasey.AI answers the enquiry, qualifies the lead, books the showing, runs the screening and gets the lease signed. It is not a PMS. Leasey handles the front of the funnel. It runs on top of Yardi, Buildium, Rent Manager or AppFolio itself, or on its own if you have no PMS.


Leasey.AI automates the leasing funnel specifically: listing syndication to 48+ marketplaces, AI-powered lead qualification across SMS, email, and Facebook Marketplace via Liza (its 24/7 phone and chat agent), autonomous tour scheduling with SMS confirmation, tenant screening (SingleKey for Canada, US providers for American properties), and lease signing with native eSignature.

Their team went above and beyond during onboarding, and the dashboard is simple and intuitive. We're saving hours every week on showing coordination while also filtering out unqualified applicants. The distinction is categorical. AppFolio runs your whole business. Leasey.AI runs your leasing funnel and integrates with the PMS that runs the rest.




When is AppFolio the right call?


AppFolio fits best when your bottleneck is operational breadth rather than leasing speed specifically. Choose AppFolio when:


You need a single system of record for accounting, maintenance, owner reporting, and leasing, and you are willing to standardise your entire operation on one platform.


You do not already have a PMS you are committed to, and you want one tool for everything from the first dollar of rent collected to the last maintenance ticket closed.


Your team's pain is not "we cannot respond to leads fast enough" but "we have six tools and nothing talks to each other across accounting, maintenance, and leasing."


AppFolio has years of product depth across these categories. According to Propmodo's March 2026 analysis of AI adoption in proptech, the number one barrier to adoption is integration with existing systems, above cost, ROI, and training. AppFolio's appeal is that it removes the integration question entirely by bundling everything.




When is Leasey.AI the right call?


Leasey.AI fits best when your bottleneck is the leasing funnel specifically and you already have (or are keeping) a PMS for back-office operations. Choose Leasey.AI when:


You already run a PMS (Yardi, Buildium, AppFolio itself, RealPage) and do not want to rip it out. You want to automate leasing on top of it. Leasey.AI integrates with existing PMS tools via two-way sync (Yardi) or standalone mode, implemented in a day.


Your constraint is leasing speed. ShowMojo's research shows 70 percent of prospects search after hours. Frontdesk Research's 2026 State of Multifamily AI report found that AI leasing agents respond in two to four minutes and deliver a 45 percent lift in lead-to-lease conversion. If your leads go cold because nobody answers at 10pm, that is a leasing-layer problem, not a PMS problem.


You want a real AI leasing agent, not a leasing module with a chat widget. Liza handles end-to-end conversation across channels (phone, SMS, email, Facebook Marketplace), qualifies leads, answers FAQs, checks availability, and books tours autonomously. A PMS leasing module typically does not operate at that depth.


You are a Canadian operator needing native SingleKey screening, Kijiji and Realtor.ca syndication, and provincial RTA/RTB compliance. Leasey.AI is Canadian-first by design rather than US-first with Canada as a footnote.




Where do they overlap, and where do they not?


AppFolio's leasing module covers the basics: applications, screening, and lease generation. What it is not built to do is run an autonomous AI conversation with a prospect at 11pm on Facebook Marketplace, qualify them against your criteria, and book the tour without a human touching the system. That is Leasey.AI's core use case.


Conversely, Leasey.AI does not do your accounting, maintenance ticketing, or owner reporting. It is not trying to. It automates roughly 90 percent of leasing tasks while your PMS handles the rest.


The overlap is narrow: both can process applications and generate leases. The differentiation is in how leads get there (AI agent vs manual or basic automation) and what happens outside leasing (full operations vs not applicable).




Can you run both?


Yes, and many operators do. The architecture is straightforward: AppFolio (or Yardi, or Buildium) serves as the system of record for accounting, maintenance, and portfolio management. Leasey.AI sits on top as the leasing automation layer, handling everything from listing syndication to signed lease. Data flows between the two via integration.


This is not redundancy. It is specialisation. A PMS is built for operational breadth. A leasing automation platform is built for funnel speed and AI-driven lead conversion. 
Running both means each tool does what it was designed for.


Leasey.AI's pricing starts at approximately $1.50 to $5 per door per month for larger portfolios, or a flat rate of roughly $499 per month for portfolios under 100 doors. The ROI calculation is straightforward: if Liza fills vacancies 60 percent faster than manual response and your average vacant day costs $50 to $80 in lost rent, the platform pays for itself within the first month of reduced vacancy.




What we will not claim


Leasey.AI is not a better full PMS than AppFolio. AppFolio has years of product depth across accounting, maintenance, and owner operations that Leasey.AI does not attempt to match.


AppFolio is not a better AI leasing agent than Liza. That is not the category AppFolio competes in.


Honest comparison content serves buyers better than marketing claims. Property managers deserve to know which tool does what so they can make the right decision for their operation, not the decision a vendor's landing page nudges them toward.




How to decide


Ask one question: is my bottleneck the whole operation, or the leasing funnel specifically?


If your bottleneck is the whole operation and you have no PMS, evaluate AppFolio (and Buildium, Yardi, and Rent Manager).


If your bottleneck is the leasing funnel and you already have a PMS, evaluate Leasey.AI.


If your bottleneck is both, run both.




Frequently asked questions


Does Leasey.AI integrate with AppFolio?


Leasey.AI integrates with major PMS platforms including Yardi (two-way sync), Buildium, and Rent Manager. AppFolio integration depends on AppFolio's API availability. Contact Leasey.AI's team to confirm the current integration status for your specific AppFolio plan and configuration.


Is Leasey.AI a replacement for AppFolio?


No. Leasey.AI handles the front of the funnel and runs on top of AppFolio, or on its own. It handles the funnel from listing to signed lease; AppFolio handles rent collection, maintenance, accounting, and active-lease management after the signature.


Does AppFolio have an AI leasing agent?


AppFolio offers AI-assisted features within its leasing module, including automated responses. However, its core product is a full PMS, not a dedicated AI leasing platform. Liza, Leasey.AI's agent, handles phone, SMS, email, and Facebook Marketplace conversations autonomously, qualifies leads, and books tours 24/7 in English, Spanish, and French.


How much does Leasey.AI cost compared to AppFolio?


Leasey.AI's subscription is approximately $1.50 to $5 per door per month for larger portfolios, or a flat rate of roughly $499 per month for portfolios under 100 doors. AppFolio's pricing varies by plan and portfolio size. The two are additive costs if you run both, but the leasing speed gains typically offset the additional spend through reduced vacancy days.


Can I use Leasey.AI without any PMS?


Yes. Leasey.AI runs standalone for operators who do not use a traditional PMS or who manage leasing separately from their back-office system. No PMS integration is required to start. Setup takes two to three onboarding sessions with Customer Success to go live.


Who is Leasey.AI built for?


Leasey.AI serves multifamily residential owners and operators (apartment buildings and communities, 100+ doors), single-family rental and build-to-rent portfolios (100+ homes), and third-party leasing companies managing multiple owner-clients. It does not serve commercial or industrial leases.
