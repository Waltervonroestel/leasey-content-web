Zillow direct syndication is live. One click, one listing, one of the largest renter search engines on the continent.


One click publishes a listing to Zillow with the same photos, copy, and pricing it has everywhere else. Edits sync back automatically. When a unit is leased, the listing comes down.


Zillow is, depending on the market, one of the largest renter search engines on the continent. For most US operators it sits in the top three. For Canadian operators it sits behind Realtor.ca and Kijiji but still drives a meaningful share of inbound leads.


Until today, getting a listing onto Zillow from Leasey.AI meant either a paid third-party syndicator or a manual MLS submission. Now it is a toggle.


Quiet release. Big operational win.
