Why we wired Leasey.AI directly into Zillow (and what direct means for your edits)


Third-party syndicators publish your listing to Zillow on a batched schedule, not in real time. When you update a price, mark a unit as leased, or change availability, the change can take hours to propagate, sometimes most of a day. In that window, you are getting inquiries about a unit that does not exist at the price advertised.


Direct syndication propagates within minutes. Same workflow, fewer phantom inquiries.


Leasey.AI is now wired directly into Zillow. One toggle in the listing settings.
