The interesting thing is not what we shipped. It is what this enables to disappear.


The "post the same unit on six tabs" ritual is one of the oldest workflows in property management. It exists because the marketplaces never agreed on a common format and the syndication tools never covered the right ones. Operators have absorbed that cost for over a decade.


What changes now is that the cost moves. From operator time to platform integration. Where it should have been all along.
