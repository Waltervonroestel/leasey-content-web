Tour friction is one of the most underrated leasing leaks. The Rently 2026 Renter Touring Expectations Report (https://rently.com/blog/2026-renter-touring-expectations) found that 47% of renters chose or seriously considered a less-than-ideal rental simply because it was easier to tour.


That same friction shows up post-booking. Prospects book on Monday for a Wednesday tour. By Tuesday night they have forgotten the address, the time, or the agent's name. No reminder lands. They do not show.


We just shipped the Showing Confirmation Dashboard. Automated reminder ahead of the tour with a one-tap reconfirm or reschedule link. Calendar updates automatically.


An automated reconfirmation workflow turns "send the reminder" from a thing nobody has time for into a thing that happens automatically.


The compounding effect across a leasing season is the part that surprises people.
