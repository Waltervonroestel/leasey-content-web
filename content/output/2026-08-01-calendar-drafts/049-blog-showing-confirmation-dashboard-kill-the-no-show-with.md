<h2>Why Do No-Shows Happen?</h2>


<p>No-shows happen because the confirmation workflow lives in someone's head rather than in a system. The first booking is rarely the problem. The problem starts when the coordinator is juggling 40 booked tours a week and the reminder workflow depends on manual effort.</p>


<p>Tours that are not actively reconfirmed turn into no-shows. Tours that get a friction-free reminder show up. According to ShowMojo, 70% of prospects search online for rental units outside normal business hours. Many of those after-hours bookings are made impulsively, and without a confirmation touchpoint, the prospect forgets or double-books by the time the tour date arrives.</p>


<p>"Send a reminder" sounds simple. It is not, when there is no system pushing it automatically. That is the gap the Showing Confirmation Dashboard closes.</p>


<h2>What Does the Showing Confirmation Dashboard Do?</h2>


<p>The dashboard automates three things that most leasing teams currently handle manually or not at all. First, it sends an SMS reminder 24 hours before the tour, with the address, the agent's name, and a one-tap reconfirm link. The prospect taps to confirm, and the tour status updates in the dashboard instantly.</p>


<p>Second, if the prospect cannot make it, a one-tap reschedule link offers the next three available slots. No phone tag, no email chain, no coordinator manually checking the calendar. The prospect picks a new time, the calendar updates, and a new confirmation SMS fires for the rescheduled tour.</p>


<p>Third, the dashboard surfaces tours where the prospect has not reconfirmed within four hours of the tour time. The coordinator can see at a glance which tours are at risk, call the prospect directly, or rebook the slot for a new prospect. The result: confirmed tours actually happen, and unconfirmed tours get rescheduled or the slot gets reused, instead of becoming dead time.</p>
