Founder reflection: building for trust in a market that has lost it


How do you confirm the person at the door is who they said they were, without slowing the entire funnel? Most operators do not. They rely on a name and a phone number.


ID Verification shipped this week. Biometric check, ~90 seconds, optional gating before a tour confirms.


What took the longest was not building it. It was getting the fair housing model right.


The verification surfaces three things and only three: verified, not verified, pending. The operator decides on facts they had access to anyway.


A small product moment. The kind that reshapes how a coordinator runs the door.
