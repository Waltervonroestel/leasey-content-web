The rebuilt Calendar is live. Split layout, overlap tooltips, and a leasing team that actually lives in the calendar.


The big changes:


1. Split layout. Multiple agents' calendars side by side, drag-to-reschedule across agents.
2. Overlap tooltips. When two tours conflict, hover surfaces both, click to reassign.
3. Drag-to-reschedule with automatic SMS update to the prospect.


The reason we rebuilt the calendar: leasing teams live in it. Every operational decision (which tour to take, which agent to assign, where to fit a walk-in) starts on the calendar screen. Old version made those decisions slower than they should have been.


Quiet but operationally important release.
