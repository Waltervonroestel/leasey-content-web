<h2>Why Did the Calendar Need Rebuilding?</h2>


<p>The calendar needed rebuilding because leasing teams running 80+ showings per week were hitting friction points that compounded across hundreds of decisions. Which tour to take, which agent to assign, where to fit a walk-in, how to handle a conflict between two last-minute bookings. Every one of those decisions starts on the calendar, and every second of friction in the calendar workflow multiplies across the week.</p>


<p>According to the Rently 2026 Renter Touring Expectations Report, 47% of renters chose or seriously considered a less-than-ideal rental simply because it was easier to tour. Tour friction on the operator side (slow reassignment, missed conflicts, manual rescheduling) directly contributes to the renter-side friction that costs leases. The rebuilt calendar removes those friction points systematically.</p>


<h2>What Is the Split Layout?</h2>


<p>The split layout lets multi-agent leasing teams view two to eight agents' calendars side by side. Each agent gets a column. You see the full team's availability at a glance, rather than clicking through individual calendars one at a time.</p>


<p>Drag a tour from one column to another to reassign it to a different agent. The SMS update to the prospect fires automatically, informing them of their new tour contact. No manual message, no email chain, no risk of the prospect showing up expecting the wrong agent. For teams running centralised leasing across multiple properties, the split layout makes cross-property and cross-agent coordination visual and immediate.</p>


<h2>What Are Overlap Tooltips?</h2>


<p>Overlap tooltips solve the scheduling conflict problem that every leasing team with real tour volume encounters. When two tours conflict (an unavoidable reality of last-minute bookings and rescheduled tours), hovering on the conflict surfaces both bookings with a one-click reassign or reschedule option.</p>


<p>In the previous calendar, conflicts were easy to miss. A coordinator would discover the overlap when two prospects showed up at the same unit at the same time, or when an agent realised mid-morning that they had been double-booked. The overlap tooltip makes conflicts visible before they become problems, and the one-click resolution means the coordinator fixes it in seconds rather than making phone calls.</p>


<h2>How Does Drag-to-Reschedule Work?</h2>


<p>Moving a tour to a new time on the calendar triggers the SMS confirmation flow automatically. The prospect receives the updated time with a one-tap reconfirm link. No manual message needed. The prospect taps to confirm the new time, and the tour status updates in the calendar and the Showing Confirmation Dashboard simultaneously.</p>


<p>This matters because rescheduling is one of the most common calendar operations. According to ShowMojo, 70% of prospects search online for rental units outside normal business hours. Many after-hours bookings need to be adjusted once the leasing team reviews the next day's schedule. Drag-to-reschedule turns what was a multi-step process (check availability, call or message the prospect, wait for confirmation, update the calendar) into a single drag action with automated follow-up.</p>


<h2>What Is Walk-In Slot Creation?</h2>


<p>Walk-in slot creation addresses the reality that not every prospect books online. Click an empty calendar slot to instantly create a tour with placeholder fields. Fill in the prospect details later, after the walk-in has happened. This is useful for in-person walk-ins where the prospect has not booked through Liza or any syndicated marketplace.</p>


<p>The walk-in slot captures the tour in the system so it counts toward showing metrics, the unit's availability is updated, and any follow-up (application link, screening request) can be triggered from the same workflow as AI-booked tours. Without this feature, walk-ins exist outside the system, creating gaps in reporting and follow-up.</p>

<h3>How many agents can the split layout display?</h3>

<h3>Does drag-to-reschedule notify the prospect automatically?</h3>
<p>Yes. When you drag a tour to a new time slot, the system automatically sends the prospect an SMS with the updated time and a one-tap reconfirm link. The prospect confirms the new time, and the calendar and Showing Confirmation Dashboard update simultaneously. No manual messaging required.</p>

<p>When two tours conflict on the calendar, hovering over the conflict displays both bookings with one-click options to reassign or reschedule either tour. <p>Yes. The split layout shows agents across multiple properties in one view. Tours from all properties appear on the same calendar.
