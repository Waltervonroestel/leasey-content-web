<h1>The leasing back-office gets a cleanup: new filters, faster invoice downloads, fewer reconciliation tickets</h1>


<p>According to the AppFolio 2025 Property Management Benchmark Report (https://naahq.org/flat-rents-ai-adoption-2025-appfolio-property-management-benchmark-report-reveals-key-trends), AI adoption in property management rose from 21% to 34% year over year. Frontdesk Research's 2026 State of Multifamily AI report (https://www.myaifrontdesk.com/multifamily/reports/2026-state-of-multifamily-ai) separately found that 85% of adopters report measurable lead-to-lease improvements. But while most of that adoption targets the front of the leasing funnel, the back-office reconciliation workflow quietly consumes three to six hours per portfolio per month for the mid-market property managers we work with. Most of that time is not analytical work. It is the friction of pulling transaction lists from one tool, exporting invoices from another, and stitching them together for the accounting team.</p>


<p>The new Billing tab on Leasey.AI is the back-office release that closes that gap.</p>


<h2>What Is New in the Billing Tab</h2>


<p>The Billing tab introduces four features designed to eliminate the manual reconciliation workflow that eats hours every month-end. Each addresses a specific friction point that property managers flagged during our Q2 2026 operator interviews.</p>


<h3>Transaction Filters</h3>


<p>Transaction filters let you slice the transaction log by date range, status (paid, pending, refunded, disputed), unit, prospect, or amount range. Filter combinations save as reusable views, accessible from the dashboard. For a property manager reconciling a 200-unit portfolio, this replaces the export-to-spreadsheet-and-sort workflow that typically takes 45 minutes per cycle. The filters work in real time across your full transaction history, so month-end becomes a five-minute check instead of a half-day project.</p>


<h3>CSV Export</h3>


<p>Any filtered view exports to CSV with one click. Headers match the column structure your accounting team uses, which eliminates the reformatting step that turns a simple export into a 20-minute task. We added direct integration with QuickBooks Online and Xero for operators who asked for it. The integration pushes transaction data directly into your accounting platform, removing the CSV intermediary entirely for teams already on those tools.</p>


<h3>One-Click Invoice Downloads</h3>


<p>Invoice PDFs download individually or as a batch. Selected invoices zip together automatically. This stops the per-invoice download dance that operators with audit requirements know too well. For a portfolio running 80 monthly transactions, batch download saves roughly 30 minutes of clicking compared to the one-at-a-time alternative.</p>


<h3>Audit Trail</h3>


<p>Every transaction touch (who edited, when, what changed) sits in the audit panel of the transaction view. For operators who face regular audits, whether from ownership groups, lenders, or regulatory bodies, the audit trail provides the documentation that previously required manual logging. The trail is immutable and timestamped, meeting the evidentiary standard most auditors require.</p>
