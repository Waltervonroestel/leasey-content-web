The US supply wave is a leasing-operations problem disguised as a pricing problem


Sun Belt metros that overbuilt (Austin, Phoenix, Denver, Tampa) are seeing the steepest rent declines in the country. The reflex is to drop asking rent to compete.


But a rent cut follows the lease for its whole term. A vacancy day is a one-time cost. Compressing days-on-market at held rent protects more yield than discounting to fill fast.


With 2026 US asking rents forecast to grow just 2.3% (Yardi Matrix), there is no margin to give away.


The lever is operational: reach, response speed, tour follow-through. That is the part an operator actually controls in an oversupplied market.
