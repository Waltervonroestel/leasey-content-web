Founder reflection: building for a cooling market


The thesis then was scalability: how do you run leasing operations when demand is so high that responsiveness alone is your differentiator?


The thesis now is the inverse: how do you run leasing operations when vacancy is rising and the lever you control is days-on-market, not rent?


The product answers turn out to be the same. Faster response, broader syndication, fewer no-shows, less administrative friction. The features that helped operators capitalise on a hot market are the same ones that protect yield in a cooling one.


That convergence is the part that surprised me. Good operational systems are market-neutral.
