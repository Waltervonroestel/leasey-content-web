"AI leasing agent" is overloaded. Here is the test that separates real ones from chat widgets.


Three things get called that. They are not the same.


1. A chat widget that answers FAQ from a knowledge base. Useful, narrow, not really an agent.
2. A scheduling tool that takes a tour booking via a chat-like interface. Useful, narrow, not really AI.
3. An actual leasing agent: handles lead qualification, schedules tours, answers contextual questions across channels, hands off to humans only when needed.


The buyer test is simple: ask the vendor to walk through a full prospect-to-tour conversation across two channels. If it cannot maintain context, it is not an agent.


We built Liza around that bar. Most of the market did not.
