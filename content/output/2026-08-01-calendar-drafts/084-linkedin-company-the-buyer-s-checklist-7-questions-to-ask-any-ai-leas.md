The buyer's checklist: 7 questions to ask any AI leasing vendor before signing


1. Can it maintain context across SMS, email, and Facebook Marketplace?
2. Can it qualify leads with your custom questions, not just FAQ?
3. Does it schedule tours autonomously or just hand off to a calendar?
4. Does it handle reschedules and confirmations end-to-end?
5. Is the response latency under 60 seconds at any hour?
6. Who owns liability for fair housing if it gives bad guidance?
7. What does the handoff to a human leasing coordinator look like?


If a vendor answers vaguely on more than two, you are looking at a chat widget. Not an agent.
