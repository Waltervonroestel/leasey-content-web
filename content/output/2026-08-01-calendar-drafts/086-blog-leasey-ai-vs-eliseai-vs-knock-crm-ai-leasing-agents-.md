<h1>Leasey.AI vs EliseAI vs Knock CRM: AI leasing agents for multifamily, honestly compared</h1>


<p>According to the Frontdesk Research's 2026 State of Multifamily AI report Industry Report, 94% of property managers plan to adopt AI tools in their operations (Frontdesk AI, 2026). That adoption wave has pushed three names to the top of buyer conversations for AI leasing in multifamily: Leasey.AI, EliseAI, and Knock CRM. They are not the same product. 
They do not solve the same problem. Here is the honest comparison.</p>


<h2>What Each One Actually Is</h2>


<p>The first step in any comparison is understanding that these three products occupy different positions in the leasing technology stack. Comparing them feature-for-feature without understanding their core design intent leads to the wrong purchase.</p>


<p><strong>EliseAI</strong> is a conversational AI built for multifamily lead engagement. Its core strength is voice, email, and text autoresponder use cases for large multifamily portfolios. Deep integrations with major PMS providers (AppFolio, Yardi Voyager, RealPage). Mature product, enterprise-focused. 
EliseAI excels at high-volume conversational automation for portfolios with thousands of doors and dedicated leasing teams that need AI to handle the first touch.</p>


<p><strong>Knock CRM</strong> is a multifamily-native CRM with workflow automation, lead nurture, and tour scheduling. Strong in pipeline management and reporting. Less about autonomous conversation, more about giving a leasing coordinator a better workflow surface. Knock is the tool you buy when your bottleneck is visibility into pipeline health, lead source attribution, and coordinator productivity tracking.</p>


<p><strong>Leasey.AI</strong> is an AI-native leasing automation platform covering the full funnel: AI lead qualification, autonomous tour scheduling with confirmation, listing syndication to 48+ marketplaces, tenant screening, application processing, and lease signing. Leasey.AI answers the enquiry, qualifies the lead, books the showing, runs the screening and gets the lease signed. Not a PMS. Leasey handles the front of the funnel. It runs on top of Yardi, Buildium or Rent Manager, or on its own if you have no PMS.

Mid-market multifamily focus, Canadian-strong.</p>


<h2>Where EliseAI Is the Better Choice</h2>


<p>EliseAI wins in specific portfolio profiles where its enterprise-grade conversational AI and deep PMS integrations deliver the most value. If your portfolio matches these criteria, EliseAI is likely the right tool.</p>


<p><strong>Enterprise multifamily portfolios with hundreds of doors per asset.</strong> EliseAI's voice automation is purpose-built for that scale. The product handles high call volumes and complex multi-property routing that smaller platforms are not designed for.</p>


<p><strong>Operators using AppFolio, Yardi Voyager, or RealPage as the primary PMS.</strong> EliseAI's integrations with these enterprise PMS platforms are deep and mature, enabling data flows that newer entrants cannot match.</p>


<p><strong>Use cases that prioritise multi-week lead nurture sequences.</strong> If your sales cycle involves extended nurture campaigns over weeks or months rather than rapid qualification-to-tour conversion, EliseAI's nurture automation is well-suited.</p>


<h2>Where Knock CRM Is the Better Choice</h2>


<p>Knock CRM wins when the primary bottleneck is workflow visibility and reporting rather than autonomous AI conversation. Its strengths serve a different operational need.</p>


<p><strong>Operators who want a CRM-first leasing workflow.</strong> Pipeline management, reporting, and coordinator handoff are Knock's primary deliverables. If your team is staffed for full conversation handling and what you need is better tools for managing those conversations, Knock fits.</p>


<p><strong>Multifamily teams that value advanced lead reporting and dashboards.</strong> Knock's reporting depth on lead source attribution, coordinator performance, and pipeline health is purpose-built for marketing and operations leadership who need data-driven decision support.</p>


<p><strong>Portfolios where the leasing team is fully staffed.</strong> If your bottleneck is workflow visibility rather than response speed, Knock gives your existing team better tools without replacing their role in the conversation.</p>


<h2>Where Leasey.AI Is the Better Choice</h2>


<p>Leasey.AI wins for mid-market portfolios that want full-funnel automation in one workflow, especially those operating in Canada or relying heavily on marketplace channels like Facebook Marketplace.</p>


<p><strong>Mid-market multifamily portfolios (roughly 50 to 1,000 doors).</strong> The platform is designed for operators who need enterprise-grade automation without enterprise-grade complexity or pricing. At approximately $1.50 to $5 per door per month, or roughly $499 per month (Leasey.AI, 2026), the economics work for mid-market operators.</p>


<p><strong>Canadian operators.</strong> Native SingleKey screening, Realtor.ca and Kijiji syndication, and RTA/RTB compliance flows are built into the platform. In a market where Canada's vacancy reached 5.1% in Q1 2026 with Calgary at 7.3% (Yardi, 2026), Canadian-first tooling delivers more days-on-market improvement than a US-centric platform lacking these integrations.</p>


<p><strong>Operators where Facebook Marketplace is a top-three rental channel.</strong> Most Canadian metros and most US secondary markets. Liza maintains cross-channel context across SMS, email, and Marketplace, recognising the same prospect regardless of channel.</p>


<p><strong>Portfolios needing the full funnel in one platform.</strong> Syndication, AI qualification, tour scheduling with confirmation, screening, application processing, and lease signing. One workflow, one platform, one prospect record from first inquiry through signed lease.</p>


<h2>What We Will Not Claim</h2>


<p>Honest comparison requires acknowledging where each product is not the best fit. Most "comparison" content in PropTech avoids this. We think the honest version helps you make a better decision.</p>


<p>Leasey.AI is not a better enterprise voice autoresponder than EliseAI. That product category has mature competitive moats and twelve-figure portfolios behind it. If you are running 5,000+ doors and need voice-first automation, EliseAI has the head start.</p>


<p>Knock CRM is not a better AI leasing agent than Liza. That is not their primary scope. Knock is a CRM with workflow automation; Liza is an autonomous AI agent. Different tools for different bottlenecks.</p>


<p>EliseAI is not a better lightweight, full-funnel platform for mid-market portfolios. Their pricing and scope target larger operators. A 200-door portfolio does not need enterprise voice automation; it needs full-funnel automation that fits its scale and budget.</p>


<h2>How to Actually Choose</h2>


<p>The seven-question buyer test separates these three categories faster than any vendor marketing will. Apply these questions to every vendor on your shortlist:</p>


<p>1. Cross-channel context: does the tool maintain prospect identity across SMS, email, and marketplace?</p>
<p>2. Custom qualification: can you configure your own qualification criteria, or only use presets?</p>
<p>3. Autonomous scheduling: does it book tours directly, or hand off to a calendar?</p>
<p>4. Reschedule handling: end-to-end, or routed to a human?</p>
<p>5. Response latency: what happens at 11pm on a Saturday? ShowMojo data shows 70% of inquiries arrive after hours (ShowMojo, 2025).</p>
<p>6. Fair housing liability: who is liable if the AI makes a mistake? The DOJ's RealPage settlement in November 2025 and Greystar's $7 million settlement in August 2025 make this non-optional (DOJ, 2025).</p>
<p>7. Human handoff: does the coordinator get full context, or start from scratch?</p>


<p>The answers to these seven questions will tell you more about each product than any feature matrix or vendor demo.</p>
