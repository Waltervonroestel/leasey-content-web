Buyers cannot tell from vendor marketing which tool owns which part of the leasing funnel. That is the actual problem.


Take Leasey vs ShowMojo, the comparison we get asked weekly. ShowMojo is a tour scheduling and self-touring tool, deep in lockbox integration and unattended showings. Leasey.AI is a full leasing automation platform: listings, leads, qualification, tours, screening, signing.


They are not better-or-worse versions of each other. They own different parts of the funnel.


The honest buyer move is to evaluate the problem first, then the tool. If self-tours via lockbox is your bottleneck, ShowMojo. If full-funnel AI automation is your bottleneck, Leasey. Some portfolios genuinely need both.
