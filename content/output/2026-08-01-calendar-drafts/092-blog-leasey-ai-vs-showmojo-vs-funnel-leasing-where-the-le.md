<h1>Leasey.AI vs ShowMojo vs Funnel Leasing: where the leasing stack actually divides</h1>


<p>ShowMojo launched its AI Virtual Agent beta in April 2026, signalling that even scheduling-first vendors recognise the leasing automation market is moving toward full-funnel AI (ShowMojo, 2026). If you are evaluating leasing operations tools in 2026, three names get raised in different buyer conversations: Leasey.AI, ShowMojo, and Funnel Leasing. They cover different parts of the funnel. Here is the honest comparison.</p>


<h2>What Each One Actually Is</h2>


<p>Understanding the core design intent of each product is more important than comparing feature lists. These three tools were built for different primary use cases, and evaluating them as interchangeable leads to the wrong purchase.</p>


<p><strong>ShowMojo</strong> is a tour scheduling and self-touring platform. Its core competency is self-tours via lockbox or smart-lock integration: prospects book a tour, get a one-time access code, tour the property unattended. Founded in 2012, ShowMojo has twelve years of product maturity on the self-tour mechanic specifically. Widely used in single-family rental (SFR) operations. According to ShowMojo (https://hello.showmojo.com/blog/property-management-takeaways-and-tips/), 70% of prospects search online for rental units outside normal business hours, which is precisely why the self-tour model exists: it lets prospects tour without an on-site agent.</p>


<p><strong>Funnel Leasing</strong> is a multifamily-focused CRM with marketing automation, lead nurture, and lead-to-lease attribution. Strong in pipeline reporting and marketing ROI tracking. Less about autonomous agent conversation, more about giving the marketing and leasing teams shared visibility into where leads come from and how they convert. Funnel is the tool you buy when your primary question is "where are our best leads actually coming from" rather than "how do we respond faster."</p>


<p><strong>Leasey.AI</strong> is an AI-native leasing automation platform covering the full funnel: listing syndication to 48+ marketplaces, AI lead qualification across SMS, email, and Facebook Marketplace, autonomous tour scheduling with showing confirmation workflows, tenant screening, application processing, and lease signing. Leasey.AI is a cloud-based residential leasing automation platform that simplifies the end-to-end lease lifecycle for organisations that manage recurring leases. Not a PMS. Leasey is a front-office leasing automation and CRM layer that sits on top of existing PMS tools (Yardi, Buildium, Rent Manager) or runs standalone. Founded 2023, Vancouver-headquartered, mid-market multifamily focus.</p>


<h2>Where ShowMojo Wins</h2>


<p>ShowMojo wins in single-family rental portfolios where self-tours via lockbox are the dominant tour workflow. If your portfolio matches these criteria, ShowMojo has a twelve-year head start on the specific mechanic you need.</p>


<p><strong>Portfolios with 50+ scattered-site single-family units and minimal on-site leasing presence.</strong> Self-touring eliminates the need for an agent to be physically present at each showing, which is operationally essential when your units are spread across a metro rather than concentrated in a building.</p>


<p><strong>Operators using lockbox-style smart locks (RemoteLock, August, Yale, and similar).</strong> ShowMojo's hardware integrations for one-time access codes and prospect identity verification at the door are deep and mature.</p>


<p><strong>Self-touring as a marketing differentiator.</strong> The "tour any time" pitch to prospects works in markets where renters value schedule flexibility and the units are suitable for unattended access. According to the Rently 2026 Renter Touring Expectations Report (https://rently.com/blog/2026-renter-touring-expectations), 47% of renters chose a less-than-ideal rental because it was easier to find and tour (Rently, 2026). Self-touring directly addresses that friction.</p>


<h2>Where Funnel Leasing Wins</h2>


<p>Funnel Leasing wins when the primary bottleneck is marketing attribution and pipeline visibility rather than autonomous conversation or response speed.</p>


<p><strong>Operators running marketing budget across multiple campaigns.</strong> Funnel's lead source attribution tells you which campaigns are producing signed leases, not just leads. For operators spending meaningful budget on paid channels alongside free channels like Facebook Marketplace, this visibility drives better allocation decisions.</p>


<p><strong>Portfolios where the leasing team owns conversation but the marketing team owns funnel reporting.</strong> Funnel bridges the gap between marketing spend and leasing outcomes, giving both teams a shared view of the pipeline.</p>


<p><strong>Operators whose primary bottleneck is "where are our best leads actually coming from" rather than response speed.</strong> If your team responds fast enough but cannot tell which channels are worth the investment, Funnel's reporting depth is the right tool.</p>


<h2>Where Leasey.AI Wins</h2>


<p>Leasey.AI wins for multifamily portfolios, mid-market property managers (100 to 1,000+ doors), and operators who want the full leasing funnel in one system rather than stitched together across tools.</p>


<p><strong>Multifamily buildings or mixed-use portfolios with a leasing team on-site or shared across buildings.</strong> The full-funnel approach means one platform handles everything from listing syndication through signed lease.</p>


<p><strong>Operators using Yardi, Buildium, or Rent Manager as their PMS.</strong> Leasey.AI sits on top as a leasing automation layer, adding AI qualification, syndication, and tour management without replacing the PMS.</p>


<p><strong>Canadian operators.</strong> Native SingleKey screening, Kijiji and Realtor.ca syndication, and Canadian RTA/RTB compliance flows. Canada's vacancy reached 5.1% in Q1 2026 with Calgary at 7.3% (Yardi, 2026). Canadian-first tooling delivers measurable advantages in days-on-market.</p>


<p><strong>Operators where Facebook Marketplace is a top-three rental channel.</strong> Liza, the AI agent, maintains cross-channel context across SMS, email, and Marketplace, recognising the same prospect regardless of channel. Research indicates 60% or more of renters go with the first operator who responds meaningfully.</p>


<p><strong>Portfolios where AI lead qualification is more valuable than either self-tour mechanics or marketing attribution depth.</strong> Liza qualifies leads 24/7 with custom operator-defined criteria, achieving 100% response rate at any hour.</p>


<h2>Where the Funnel Breaks If You Only Have One</h2>


<p>Some portfolios have a real use case for two of these tools. The leasing funnel has distinct stages, and no single tool is best at every stage. The practical combinations we see in the market:</p>


<p><strong>Leasey.AI + ShowMojo.</strong> Leasey handles lead qualification, scheduling, screening, and signing. ShowMojo handles the physical self-tour at the door. Each tool does the job it is best at.</p>


<p><strong>Leasey.AI + Funnel Leasing.</strong> Leasey handles AI conversation and full-funnel automation. Funnel handles marketing attribution and lead source reporting. The combination gives you both operational speed and marketing visibility.</p>


<p>The pattern works because these tools occupy different positions in the stack. Choosing one does not necessarily mean excluding another, provided the integration makes operational sense for your portfolio.</p>
